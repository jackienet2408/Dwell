Welcome to Dwell

1. Pick a key
Tap a note along the top row.

2. Hold a chord
Tap a chord pad below — the pad swells in and holds under the band. Tap another to crossfade.

3. Shape the sound
Open Sound Design at the bottom to drag knobs for tone, movement, and space. Save your own presets with + Save current.

4. Stop anytime
The square button next to the master fader cuts the pad.
